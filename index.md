## 📋 Project Overview
VSM Flow Studio 2026 acts as a digital canvas for anyone working with Lean Six Sigma methods. It replaces paper-based value stream mapping with a clean browser interface. You build process maps by dragging steps onto a workspace. The program tracks your metrics and updates key performance indicators as you change your design. It saves your work directly to your computer browser cache so you do not need a server or cloud account.

## 🛠 Features
*   **Drag-and-Drop Mapping:** You place process steps on your board with mouse movements. Connection lines snap into place to keep your map organized.
*   **Live Data Monitoring:** The software recalculates cycle times, wait times, and throughput yield whenever you adjust a process parameter. 
*   **Local Storage:** Your maps remain on your local machine. You do not need to upload company data to external platforms.
*   **Lean Calculations:** The engine automates math for takt time, process efficiency, and inventory turnover.
*   **Export Options:** You can save your final maps as images for your presentations or reports.

## 🚀 Getting Started
Once you open the software, you see a blank grid. This is your workspace.

*   **Create a Map:** Look at the left sidebar for a list of process shapes. Click and hold a shape, then drag it onto the grid to place it.
*   **Link Processes:** Click the edge of one box and drag to pull a connector line toward another box. The line will lock into place.
*   **Edit Details:** Click on any box to open the settings panel on the right side. You can enter cycle time, uptime, and changeover values here.
*   **Check Metrics:** The top dashboard shows live totals for your current map. It changes color if your throughput drops below your target settings.

## 💾 Saving Your Work
The application saves your progress automatically. Whenever you modify a box or move a line, the program caches the data in your browser profile. If you close the window and return later, your map appears exactly where you left it. If you want to move your map to another machine, use the "Export to JSON" feature in the File menu, save the file to a USB drive, and use the "Import" button on your new computer.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting
If you experience trouble, try these steps:

*   **Blank Screen:** Clear your browser cache and restart the application.
*   **Slow Performance:** If your map has hundreds of steps, close other browser tabs to free up system memory.
*   **Icons Not Loading:** Disable any ad-blocking software that might interfere with the interface toolkits.
*   **Installation Error:** Ensure you have administrator rights on your computer. If the file fails to open, right-click the setup icon and select "Run as administrator."

## 📚 Lean Six Sigma Basics
This software assumes familiarity with standard Lean terminology. If you find yourself stuck, refer to these common definitions:
*   **Cycle Time:** The time to complete one process cycle.
*   **Takt Time:** The pace of customer demand divided by work time.
*   **Inventory:** The quantity of work-in-progress materials sitting between process steps.
*   **Waste:** Any activity that consumes resources without adding value to the end user.
